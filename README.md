# Turtle Games: Predictive Analytics

## Overview

Turtle Games is a global gaming company seeking to improve sales performance, customer engagement and customer satisfaction through data-driven decision making.

This project was completed as part of the London School of Economics (LSE) Data Analytics Career Accelerator and uses advanced Python and R techniques to analyse customer loyalty, behaviour, segmentation and customer feedback.

## Business Objective

The aim of the project was to:

- Analyse customer engagement and loyalty
- Identify opportunities for customer segmentation
- Predict loyalty using machine learning techniques
- Explore customer feedback through Natural Language Processing (NLP)
- Support targeted marketing and product development strategies

## Key Findings

### Loyalty Analysis

- Customer income and spending behaviour were strong predictors of loyalty.
- Multiple linear regression explained 82.1% of the variation in loyalty points.
- Income and loyalty showed a strong relationship across customer groups.

### Decision Tree Classification

- Accuracy: 99%
- Precision: 97%

The model successfully predicted customer loyalty categories and highlighted the importance of demographic characteristics.

### Customer Segmentation

Five distinct customer segments were identified:

| Cluster | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| 0 | Moderate income, moderate spending |
| 1 | High income, low spending |
| 2 | High income, high spending |
| 3 | Low income, high spending |
| 4 | Low income, low spending |

The segmentation model supports targeted marketing and resource allocation.

### Natural Language Processing

Analysis of customer reviews identified:

- Strong overall customer satisfaction
- Frequent references to product quality
- Opportunities for product improvement
- Positive sentiment associated with highly rated products

### Statistical Analysis in R

Analysis found:

- Near-normal distribution of loyalty data
- Minimal outlier influence
- Slight positive skewness
- Strong support for predictive modelling applications

## Business Recommendations

### Customer Loyalty

Develop targeted loyalty campaigns for high-value customer groups based on income, spending and engagement behaviour.

### Marketing

Use customer segmentation to personalise offers and improve campaign effectiveness.

### Product Development

Use customer feedback and sentiment analysis to identify opportunities for product improvement.

### Customer Retention

Implement decision-tree-based loyalty monitoring to identify customers at risk of disengagement.
